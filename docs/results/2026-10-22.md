The cohort figures have four rows (SHANK3 cohorts 1/2, CNTNAP2 cohorts 3/4); the family figures pool cohorts into SHANK3/CNTNAP2 rows. Columns are ABL 20/40/60. Genotype colors are **WT blue, HOM green, HET orange**.

The earlier unrestricted-RT JND fits are preserved; these figures refit the RT-window trials.

### Accuracy

At each genotype/ABL/|ILD|, combine actual positive/negative ILD trials across sessions and animals. The dot is **pooled correct choices / pooled valid choices**. The bar is sample SD (`ddof=1`) of individual-animal condition accuracies around their **unweighted mean**, divided by √animal count, and centered on the pooled dot. Trials weight the point; animals have equal weight in the SEM. These are across-animal SEMs, rather than binomial standard errors or pooled-trial SEMs.

The 795 animal accuracies produce 270 pooled points and 240 available SEMs. SHANK3 cohort 2 WT/HOM each have one animal; their 30 condition dots remain visible without bars. Missing SD/SEM values are blank, never replaced with zero. Both accuracy figures share limits 0–1, with equally spaced |ILD| labels 1/2/4/8/16 and WT/HOM/HET offsets −0.12/0/+0.12.

### Just noticeable difference

JND summarizes the whole signed-ILD psychometric curve, so each panel has **WT/HOM/HET on x**, with one pooled JND per genotype/ABL. Sum raw right/left choice counts before calculating ten signed-ILD probabilities: `P(right) = response_poke == 1 / all recorded left-or-right choices`. All selected choices have response codes ±1.

Fit `lower + (upper-lower)/(1+exp(-k*(ILD-x0)))` using unweighted `curve_fit` on the ten probabilities. Parameter order is `[upper, lower, x0, k]`, initial guess `[max(Pright), min(Pright), median(ILD), 0.1]`, asymptotes bounded 0–1, unrestricted x0, nonnegative k, and `maxfev=10000`. Calculate the unchanged paper-defined JND as half the inverse-sigmoid ILD spacing at **`y_high=0.75*upper`** and **`y_low=0.25*(1-lower)`**. Both targets must lie strictly inside the increasing fit's asymptotes, with finite positive crossing spacing.

The dot comes from the **pooled-trial fit using every selected animal**, including animals whose individual JND is undefined. The error bar is sample SD of **defined individual-animal JNDs** around their unweighted mean, divided by √defined-animal count. It is centered on the pooled JND, as requested. A pooled JND is not the average of animal JNDs; these bars describe across-animal variability and are not fit-confidence intervals for the pooled estimate. Tick labels distinguish **pool** animal count from the number contributing to **SEM**.

All 54 pooled JNDs are defined. Of 159 animal/ABL fits, 154 JNDs are defined; five have targets outside their fitted asymptotes:

| Animal | Cohort | ABL with undefined JND |
|---|---|---:|
| ASD0035 | CNTNAP2 cohort 3 | 20 |
| ASD0058 | CNTNAP2 cohort 4 | 20 |
| ASD0044 | SHANK3 cohort 1 | 40 |
| ASD0048 | SHANK3 cohort 1 | 40, 60 |

Their trials remain in every pooled fit. The 47 available JND SEMs exclude only undefined individual estimates at that ABL. Seven cohort bars are unavailable: SHANK3 cohort 2 WT/HOM at all three ABLs, and CNTNAP2 cohort 3 HET at ABL 20, where only one individual JND is defined despite two animals contributing pooled choices. Keep all seven pooled dots without bars. Both JND figures use the same y-range, including every point and full SEM bar with padding.

All panels are compact and square, with filled circular dots, capped SEM bars, and no connecting lines. Independent verification reconstructs raw counts/accuracies and memberships, refits all 213 psychometrics, checks 416 numerical threshold crossings, all 324 PDF dots and 287 bars, missing-bar handling, zero-lapse JND=ln(3)/k, and unchanged source/prior-result hashes.
