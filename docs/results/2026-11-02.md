The Gamma clock uses fixation-referenced nonnegative time. Location is fixed at zero by the user's decision; it is not fitted. Shape is dimensionless and scale is in seconds. Lapse parameters are shared between OFF and all selected ON rows within each independently fitted session type. The fitted model has eight parameters: five proactive parameters plus `lapse_prob`, `shape_lapse` and `scale_lapse`.

The exact baseline source-row manifests and timing pairs are reused: 139,547 likelihood trials for type 8 (89,880 OFF, 49,667 ON) and 93,381 for type 9 (58,706 OFF, 34,675 ON). These are trial-pooled fits of all eight animals (90, 92, 93, 98, 99, 100, 102, 103), including controls 90 and 102. Filters retain training level 16, repeat flags 0/2/NaN and LED flags 0/1. Finite event-3 aborts contribute density; finite completed success +/-1 trials contribute survival at `intended_fix`. Early aborts are retained, with no 300 ms truncation. The lapse clock starts at fixation, while the figure remains aligned to LED onset. Session type 7 is unchanged.

The likelihoods below are in-sample, evaluated at posterior-mean parameters on identical trials. Higher is better; held-out prediction was not evaluated. Exponential/uniform/Gaussian/Gamma have 7/6/8/8 fitted parameters, respectively.

| 9 | -26,771.423 | -27,619.568 | -26,771.225 | -26,767.762 | +3.660 | +3.463 |

Gamma improves type 8 over exponential by 92.304 log units, but is 10.588 log units below Gaussian. Its LED-ON displayed-window RMSE decreases by 7.76% relative to exponential (0.04319 to 0.03984 per second), while Gaussian remains slightly lower at 0.03921. Type-8 Gamma ON predicted abort fraction remains below observed (0.1982 versus 0.2085). For type 9, Gamma gains only 3.660 log units over exponential and 3.463 over Gaussian. Its ON displayed-window RMSE increases by 0.74% relative to exponential (0.04582 to 0.04615). The broad, low theoretical ON peak remains. Thus this Gamma variant does not resolve the type-9 mismatch.

Derived lapse moments are computed for every posterior draw and then averaged: mean = `shape*scale`, SD = `sqrt(shape)*scale`, mode = `max(shape-1,0)*scale`. Type 8 has a delayed Gamma peak; type 9 is close to exponential, and its shape interval includes one. The lapse probability is a mixture probability, not the observed abort fraction, because some lapse times occur after censoring.

The Gamma density is `t^(shape-1) * exp(-t/scale) / (Gamma(shape)*scale^shape)` for nonnegative fixation time. Censored lapse survival is the regularized upper incomplete Gamma `Q(shape, intended_fix/scale)`. A `1e-300` survival floor protects double-precision underflow; fitted log survivals at 2.2 s are about -29.9 and -19.1, far above the floor's log value of -690.8. No support truncation or truncation normalizer is applied. Shape hard/plausible bounds are `[0.05,50]`/`[0.3,10]`; scale bounds are `[0.001,5]`/`[0.01,0.8] s`. The common priors match earlier fits. Initialization at shape 1 and scale 0.2 s exactly reproduces the original exponential lapse start (`beta=5/s`).

Both fits completed full-rank SVI with seed 0, clipped Adam at 2e-4, 64-node proactive quadrature, 10,000 posterior draws and patience-12 restore-best stopping. Type 8 checked 150,000 steps and restored 126,000; type 9 extended to 174,000 and restored 162,000. Losses, guide states and posterior samples were finite. Largest late parameter shifts were 0.114 and 0.061 posterior SD, respectively. Both shape and scale means are more than 10 posterior SD from the nearest hard bound. Independent SciPy density/CDF/survival, normalization, shape-gradient, four-term likelihood, exponential-special-case and censoring-mass checks passed. Gamma bin-averaged curves also passed independent SciPy and mass checks. Full plotted curve areas agree with analytic abort probabilities, and all 157 historical artifact hashes are unchanged.

This plotting-only update uses the same completed fits and trial-pooled eight-animal selection documented above: 139,547 event-or-censored trials for type 8 and 93,381 for type 9, including early event-3 aborts and all selected LED-ON rows. One lapse PDF per session type/family serves both OFF and ON because lapse parameters were shared in the fit. Each parameter is averaged over the saved posterior draws before evaluating the density; this is a plug-in density at mean parameters, rather than an average of densities across posterior draws. The Gaussian PDF includes its conditioning normalizer for nonnegative times; Gamma location remains fixed at zero.

Title `p` denotes the mixture lapse probability. The purple PDF integrates to one over its full support independently of `p`. It does not include the trial-specific stimulus-censoring mask used in the adjacent observed-abort panels. The uniform model therefore has the same flat conditional PDF in both session types despite their different fitted lapse probabilities. Only 22.73% of its full probability mass lies in the displayed 0–0.5 s window; this display choice does not change its fitted 0–2.2 s support. The density audit records displayed mass for every family/type.

All 16 abort data/model curve pairs are exactly reused from `fitting_aborts/numpyro_svi_led7_s8_s9_aggregate_no_trunc_gamma_lapse_outputs/summary_figures/comparison_payload.pkl`. All 190 existing source fit/comparison/asset files were hash-verified unchanged. The eight lapse densities agree with independent SciPy distributions to less than 1.1e-14 per second over the plotted grid, with unit full-support mass. No posterior, likelihood, source-row selection, normalization or convergence result changed. The rendered figure was visually inspected.
