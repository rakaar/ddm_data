### Selection and denominator

These four rows correspond to the earlier [four-row figure](../assets/results/2026-10-01/led7_s7_s8_sequence_abort_pre_onset_matched_summary_4x4.png): s7/s8 abort→abort, then s7/s8 isolated valid→abort→valid. The **selected aborts have not changed**. For abort→abort, the current event-3 trial must immediately follow an event-3 trial with an actual `trial` ID one lower in the same animal/session; `A₁→A₂→A₃` selects A₂ and A₃. Isolated selections have immediately adjacent valid (`success ∈ {-1,+1}`) neighbors. Neighboring LED flags do not restrict selection. Labels are established on the original trial stream and frozen before matching.

**Every rate denominator is now all eligible current trials** (`abort_event == 3` or `success ∈ {-1,+1}`) in the corresponding animal × session type × current `LED_trial` group, regardless of neighboring outcomes. The top rows previously required a preceding abort in the denominator; the bottom rows already used all eligible trials. Columns 1–2 remain the selected-abort `intended_fix` and fixation-referenced `LED_onset_time` unit-area densities with 40 ms bins. Columns 3–4 use 20 ms bins and divide each finite selected-abort bin count by that animal's complete eligible-trial count and bin width. Missing `timed_fix` rows stay in the denominator and selected-timing count but cannot enter the LED-aligned histogram.

The complete aligned histogram area is the frequency of this selected abort category among eligible trials, not a conditional probability given the neighboring outcomes. Curves and shaded bands are the **equal-animal mean ± sample-SD/√6 SEM** over animals 92, 93, 98, 99, 100 and 103. Displayed `n` and `N` sum trial counts across animals, whereas the plotted area averages the six animal fractions rather than pooling `n/N`. The full bins span −2 to +2 s; both rate columns display −0.4 to +0.5 s and share one y-scale across all rows. OFF onset is scheduled/counterfactual.

### Matching with the corrected denominator

The original top-row selection could not simply be rescaled: with an all-eligible denominator, its saved pre-onset OFF/ON differences exceeded the matching tolerances. The top rows were optimized again, independently for each animal, using 20 ms bins over [−2, 0) s. A certified SciPy integer optimization maximized retained selected pre-onset aborts under its established conservative linear rule. It requires the final OFF/ON difference to be ≤0.005 s⁻¹ in every pre-onset bin and ≤0.001 in total pre-onset fraction. Within each optimized bin, intact source rows were sampled without replacement with seed `20261001` reset for each session type. Only selected pre-onset aborts were removed; all valid, other eligible, zero-time, post-onset and missing-time rows were retained. The bottom-row matched selections were reused by exact source-row identity, so every bottom-row numerical curve and SEM is unchanged.

| s7 abort→abort | 63,525 / 30,910 | 63,357 / 30,444 | 1,360 / 1,103 | 634 |
| s8 abort→abort | 68,584 / 38,486 | 68,542 / 37,700 | 921 / 1,677 | 828 |
| s7 valid→abort→valid | 63,525 / 30,910 | 62,399 / 30,689 | 5,372 / 3,144 | 1,347 |
| s8 valid→abort→valid | 68,584 / 38,486 | 67,024 / 38,340 | 4,846 / 3,959 | 1,706 |

The twelve new top-row optimizations were certified optimal under the stated rule. Their largest animal-level per-bin differences were **0.004760 s⁻¹** (s7) and **0.004727 s⁻¹** (s8); largest total pre-onset fraction differences were **0.000904** and **0.000686**. The two selected aborts missing `timed_fix` in each top row were retained in the denominators and selected-timing distributions. All six animals contribute to every curve.

The source script verifies actual adjacent trial IDs, original numerator identities, full histogram integrals, saved bottom-row curves, unchanged top-row timing curves, protected rows, denominator totals, matching tolerances and equal-animal means/SEMs. It saves four kept/removed source-row manifests, bin quotas, solver and retention audits, per-panel count/area and exact curve audits, an NPZ with means/SEMs, and a run summary under `fitting_aborts/led7_s7_s8_sequence_abort_all_eligible_denominator_*`. The matching uses observed abort outcomes and is descriptive; pre-onset overlap alone does not establish a causal LED effect.

### Conditional definitions

The rows are s7/s8 aborts immediately following aborts, then s7/s8 isolated valid→abort→valid trials. Let **A** mean `abort_event == 3` and **V** mean `success ∈ {-1,+1}`. For the top rows, the numerator is AA and the denominator is **AA + AV**: among eligible current trials immediately after an abort, how often is the current trial another abort? Every adjacent pair counts, so A₁→A₂→A₃→V contributes A₂ and A₃ to the numerator and A₂, A₃ and V to the denominator. For the bottom rows, the numerator is VAV and the denominator is **VAV + VVV**: among eligible current trials with valid trials immediately before and after, how often is the current trial an abort?

All selections require actual adjacent `trial` IDs within the same animal and session. OFF/ON is assigned using the **current trial's own LED flag**, with no restriction on neighboring LED flags. The analysis uses the standardized s7/s8 CSVs with their existing training-level-16 and repeat-trial filters, and animals 92, 93, 98, 99, 100 and 103. `LED_onset_time` is already fixation-referenced; for OFF it is the scheduled/counterfactual onset.

The two s7 OFF abort-after-abort trials and two s8 ON abort-after-abort trials with missing `timed_fix` remain selected and in the denominator, including after matching. They enter the intended-fixation and onset distributions but cannot enter the aligned-time histogram. Thus the rate-panel `n` annotations are two lower for those groups than the selected counts in the table.

### Curves, matching and checks

Columns 1–2 show selected-abort unit-area densities with 40 ms bins. Columns 3–4 use 20 ms bins, dividing each animal's selected finite-abort bin count by its corresponding conditional N and bin width. Curves average the six animal-normalized histograms equally; the bands are sample-SD/√6 SEM. The full rate-histogram area is the **mean of the six finite-abort fractions**. Displayed `n` and `N` sum counts across animals, so their pooled ratio need not equal the displayed mean area. Full rate bins span −2 to +2 s; the displayed range is −0.4 to +0.5 s, with a common scale for both rate columns.

This new four-row view reuses the exact curves and retained-row identities from `fitting_aborts/plot_led7_s7_s8_sequence_abort_valid_neighbor_conditioning.py`. In that analysis, the top-row matching used AA+AV, and the bottom-row matching was recalculated using VAV+VVV. The saved selections retain 1,674/3,097 and 2,287/3,426 selected aborts in the top rows, and 8,612/9,863 and 9,107/10,511 in the bottom rows. Only selected pre-onset aborts were removed, with sequence labels frozen before selection. The independently verified per-animal OFF/ON differences remain ≤0.005 s⁻¹ in every 20 ms pre-onset bin and ≤0.001 in total pre-onset fraction. No new subsampling or fit was performed for this four-row view.

The new script independently checks the saved source-row identities and adjacent-trial conditions against the CSVs, reconstructs every histogram, verifies the original and retained denominators, and checks full areas and equal-animal means/SEMs against the saved curves.
