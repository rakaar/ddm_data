# Results: 2026-09-26

These four figures pool animals **92, 93, 98, 99, 100, and 103**, with equal weight per trial. Controls **90 and 102 are excluded before calculating any onset cutoffs or denominators**. Rows are LED7 session types 7, 8, and 9; the figures use 2, 3, 4, or 6 onset groups in columns.

### Timing coordinate and grouping

The source is the standardized all-trial `raw_data/LED7_s7.csv`, `LED7_s8.csv`, and `LED7_s9.csv`. Their single `LED_onset_time` column is measured **from fixation onset, t = 0**. For s7/s8, the exporter stored `intended_fix - raw MAT LED_onset_time`; for s9, it stored the raw MAT `LED_onset_time`. The CSV onset values were checked against `outMatrix_LED7_latest.mat::totalout_stGtACRII` and agree exactly with these definitions. The plotting script uses the CSV column directly, without another subtraction. Abort times on the x-axis are `timed_fix - LED_onset_time`.

For each requested group count, quantile cutoffs are calculated **separately within each session type**, after control exclusion and before selecting aborts. OFF and ON trials are pooled to obtain shared cutoffs, then plotted separately within each interval. Thus a given OFF/ON panel uses the same onset range; the two LED conditions need not have equal trial counts. Groups have approximately equal pooled counts: ties remain together, and the largest difference is four trials. No row is discarded to enforce equal group sizes.

Filters are `training_level == 16`, `repeat_trial ∈ {0, 2, NaN}`, and `LED_trial ∈ {0, 1}`; there are no missing LED or repeat values in these source subsets. Fixation aborts are `abort_event == 3`. Every retained trial remains in its group denominator, including other outcomes and aborts missing `timed_fix`. Each curve uses `bin_count / (all group trials × 0.020 s)`; its full area equals the finite plotted-abort fraction. The nine missing-timing aborts below cannot enter the curves. No success, LED-power, or model-likelihood filter is applied.

These are descriptive onset-group comparisons: `intended_fix` is not matched across groups. Exact onset cutoffs, counts, missing timings, fractions, and timing medians are saved in `fitting_aborts/led7_s7_s8_s9_no_controls_abort_rates_by_onset_quantiles_audit.csv`.
