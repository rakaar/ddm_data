### Reused psychometric fits and JND definition

P(right) is recorded right choices (`response_poke=1`) divided by all recorded
left/right choices at each signed ILD and ABL. Reuse the saved four-parameter
sigmoid:

```text
lower + (upper - lower) / (1 + exp(-k * (ILD - x0)))
```

The original fits used unweighted `curve_fit` on the ten ILD probability
points, parameter order `[upper,lower,x0,k]`, initial
`[max(P),min(P),median(ILD),0.1]`, asymptotes bounded to 0–1, unrestricted x0,
nonnegative k, and `maxfev=10000`. All displayed points and parameters are
reused without refitting.

Keep the existing paper-based JND definition:

```text
y_high = 0.75 * upper
y_low = 0.25 * (1 - lower)
JND = (inverse_sigmoid(y_high) - inverse_sigmoid(y_low)) / 2
```

Both thresholds must lie strictly within the fitted asymptotes. The lower
target is not changed to a fraction of the fitted dynamic range.

### Per-animal ΔCDF cutoffs

For each animal and |ILD|, calculate:

\[
D(t)=\frac{|F_{20}(t)-F_{40}(t)|+|F_{40}(t)-F_{60}(t)|+|F_{20}(t)-F_{60}(t)|}{3}.
\]

Match the original Figure 1 moving-window onset rule: at bin index `i`,
compare **the mean of the next ten bins**, `D[i+1:i+11]`, with **the mean
of the preceding ten bins**, `D[i-10:i]`. Exclude the current bin. The first
index with a rise **≥0.005** supplies the cutoff, at that bin's center.
This is a rise between two window means, rather than a direct crossing of
`D(t)=0.005`. The two windows are each 10 ms wide.

All **265 freshly calculated per-animal/|ILD| cutoffs** are available, ranging
from **10.5 to 104.5 ms**. These replace the original paper's hard-coded
across-animal cutoffs for this extension only. The ΔCDF panel overlays the
five |ILD|-colored curves and matching dashed cutoff markers; its title lists
all five cutoff times in **milliseconds**. Cutoff markers appear only in this
second column.

### Empirical quantiles and Figure 1 rescaling

Display **10%, 30%, 50%, 70%, and 90%** empirical RT quantiles using linear
interpolation, against |ILD| **1/2/4/8/16** with logarithmic x spacing. The
three raw ABL panels use consistent quantile colors and marker shapes.
The scaled overlay uses ABL colors and the same quantile marker shapes.
Points are connected across |ILD|; no animal averaging or quantile SEMs are
introduced. The four quantile panels share a y-range within each animal,
covering all raw and scaled values without clipping.

For every animal/|ILD|, use **ABL 60 as reference** and fine quantiles from
**1% through 99%** to obtain the ABL-20 and ABL-40 factors. The fitting mask
includes reference ABL-60 quantiles between the detected cutoff `t0` and
**0.3 s**. The source Figure 1 calculation is preserved:

```text
x = Q60 - t0
y = Qabl - Q60
m = sum(x * (y - y_first)) / sum(x * x)
factor = 1 + m
```

`y_first` is the first difference within the fitting mask. The parameter
`m` here is a quantile-scaling fit coefficient, distinct from psychometric
slope `k`. For plotting quantiles above the cutoff:

\[
Q_{\mathrm{scaled}}=t_0+\frac{Q-t_0}{1+m}.
\]

Quantiles at or below the cutoff are unchanged; all ABL-60 quantiles remain
unchanged. All **530 scaling fits** are valid, with factors from approximately
**0.964 to 4.360**. An unavailable cutoff or nonfinite/nonpositive factor
would be recorded explicitly and omit the affected scaled values; an
unsuccessful fit is never silently displayed as unchanged successful scaling.
