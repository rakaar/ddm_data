*Animal-wise posterior means and 95% credible intervals for the six LED7 animals. Red open circles show the paper-era three-parameter proactive VBMC fits; LED7/93 now uses the converged corrected-bound rerun promoted to the canonical fit pickle on 2026-08-29, replacing its former maximum-evaluation posterior and broad delay interval. The SVI baseline parameters are `V_A_base` and `theta_A`, and its total proactive delay is computed draw by draw as `del_a_minus_del_LED + del_m_plus_del_LED`. The parameter correspondence remains informative but not likelihood-matched: paper VBMC uses the 300 ms-truncated three-parameter proactive model, whereas the SVI model includes LED step-jump dynamics, lapses, and no truncation. Selected SVI sources use the 150k audits for animals 92, 99, 100, and 103 and the completed original fits for 93 and 98.*
