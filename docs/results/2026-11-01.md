The fits retain the original eight animals (90, 92, 93, 98, 99, 100, 102, 103), training level 16, repeat-trial values 0/2/NaN, and LED flags 0/1. Exact saved source-row manifests and timing pairs are reused: 139,547 likelihood trials for session type 8 and 93,381 for session type 9. Finite event-3 aborts contribute density; completed success +/-1 trials contribute survival at `intended_fix`. Early aborts remain included. Session type 7 is unchanged.
The uniform model refits all five proactive parameters and one shared OFF/ON lapse probability; the exponential baseline has an additional lapse-rate parameter. Its lapse survival at stimulus onset is `max(1 - intended_fix/2.2, 0)`. Both uniform fits use the original priors for shared parameters, deterministic initialization, seed 0, full-rank SVI, and patience-12 restore-best stopping after at least 150,000 steps.
The fixed uniform variant did not improve overall fit quality in these runs. The table reports in-sample log likelihood evaluated at posterior-mean parameters; higher is better. Held-out prediction was not evaluated.
| Session type | Trials | Exponential log likelihood | Uniform log likelihood | Uniform minus exponential |

| 8 | 139,547 | -39,934.550 | -40,145.430 | -210.880 |
| 9 | 93,381 | -26,771.423 | -27,619.568 | -848.145 |

The session-type-8 ON displayed-window histogram RMSE decreases only about 0.6% (0.04319 to 0.04293 per second); its OFF error increases. Both OFF and ON errors increase for session type 9, and the ON peak remains too broad and low. The full condition audit is available below.

Both fits completed with finite losses and posterior samples. Restored-best/final-checked steps are 104,000/150,000 for type 8 and 139,000/151,000 for type 9. The largest late parameter shift is 0.12 posterior standard deviations. Independent SciPy density/survival checks, unit-mass and censoring checks, mixture-likelihood checks, and finite-gradient checks passed. All 42 historical fit/diagnostic/figure hashes, including the session-type-7 fit, remain unchanged.

## LED7 session types 8 and 9: Gaussian lapses truncated at fixation start

Source: `fitting_aborts/plot_led7_s8_s9_exponential_vs_uniform_lapse.py`
Figure: `docs/assets/results/2026-11-01/led7_s8_s9_exponential_uniform_gaussian_lapse_abort_rate_3x2.png`

All five proactive parameters and the three lapse parameters (`lapse_prob`, `mu_lapse`, `sigma_lapse`) were fitted jointly, giving eight free parameters per session type. Lapse parameters are shared between OFF and all ON rows within a session type. Mu and sigma belong to the underlying, untruncated Gaussian. The actual retained lapse-time mean and SD are derived sample-wise from its positive-time tail and then averaged over posterior draws.

The same eight animals (90, 92, 93, 98, 99, 100, 102, 103), training level 16, repeat flags 0/2/NaN, LED flags 0/1, exact source-row manifests, and timing pairs are retained. Type 8 has 139,547 likelihood trials and type 9 has 93,381. Finite event-3 aborts contribute density, and completed success +/-1 trials contribute survival at `intended_fix`. Early aborts remain included. Session type 7 and the historical exponential/uniform results are unchanged.

The Gaussian lapse density includes the normalizer `Phi(mu/sigma)` for conditioning on fixation time >= 0. Censored lapse survival is `Phi((mu - intended_fix)/sigma) / Phi(mu/sigma)`. The user allowed negative underlying mu. Hard bounds are `mu in [-5,5] s` and `sigma in [0.001,5] s`, with trapezoid plausible intervals `[-0.5,0.5] s` and `[0.01,0.8] s`. Shared priors and fitting settings match the preceding analyses.

The following likelihoods are in-sample, evaluated at posterior-mean parameters on identical trials. Higher is better; held-out prediction was not evaluated.

| Session type | Exponential log likelihood | Uniform log likelihood | Gaussian log likelihood | Gaussian minus exponential |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 | -39,934.550 | -40,145.430 | -39,831.658 | +102.892 |
| 9 | -26,771.423 | -27,619.568 | -26,771.225 | +0.198 |

Gaussian lapses improve the type-8 in-sample likelihood by 102.892 log units and reduce LED-ON displayed-window histogram RMSE by 9.2% (0.04319 to 0.03921 per second). OFF RMSE changes by less than 0.4%. The type-8 ON predicted abort fraction remains below the observed fraction (0.1982 versus 0.2085), so the fit remains imperfect. Type 9 is effectively unchanged: +0.198 log units, with ON RMSE changing by less than 0.03%. The broad, low theoretical ON peak remains.

| Session type | Underlying mu (ms) | Underlying sigma (ms) | Retained lapse mean (ms) | Retained lapse SD (ms) | Lapse probability (%) |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 8 | 47.2 | 74.0 | 80.0 | 53.6 | 2.364 |
| 9 | -1176.2 | 405.4 | 118.6 | 110.7 | 5.509 |

The negative underlying mu for type 9 places the untruncated Gaussian center before fixation start; the fitted lapse distribution uses its nonnegative-time tail. Its retained mean is positive, as shown above. Posterior intervals for all fitted parameters are available in the parameter CSV.

Both fits completed the original full-rank SVI configuration (seed 0, clipped Adam at 2e-4, 64-node proactive quadrature, 10,000 posterior samples), with 150,000 checked steps and patience-12 restore-best stopping. Best checkpoints were 126,000 for type 8 and 109,000 for type 9. Losses, guide states, and posterior samples were finite. The largest late parameter shifts were 0.126 and 0.233 posterior SD. No fitted Gaussian mean was within two posterior SD of a hard bound; the closest mu/sigma hard bound was more than 11 posterior SD away. Independent SciPy log-density/log-survival, normalization, negative-mu/narrow-sigma, gradient, mixture-likelihood, and censoring-mass checks passed. Full plotted mass agrees with analytic abort probabilities. All 86 historical artifact hashes were preserved.

Fit root: `fitting_aborts/numpyro_svi_led7_s8_s9_aggregate_no_trunc_gaussian_lapse_outputs/`, with `session_type_8/`, `session_type_9/`, `summary_figures/`, and the exact fit-code snapshot in `source/`.

Fit driver: `fitting_aborts/numpyro_svi_proactive_led_step_jump_no_trunc_exp_lapse_led7_s8_s9_aggregate.py`, using `LED7_S8_S9_AGG_SVI_LAPSE_FAMILY=gaussian`.
